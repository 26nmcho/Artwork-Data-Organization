> **high restrictions context for project:** This fragment of painted papyrus combines dense hieratic text with a vividly rendered funerary vignette characteristic of New Kingdom Book of the Dead manuscripts. > **less restriction alt text method:** A long, narrow strip of aged brown papyrus fills the image, roughly six times wider than tall, with frayed edges and darker patches of wear. In front of him a standing robed woman raises her hands in a gesture of reverence; a low offering table with small items and a short plant sit between them. Above the scene is a rectangular panel of larger hieroglyphic signs. Overall the piece combines text and a ritual image, faded but with traces of blue, red, green and black pigment.

> **plain description:** This horizontal strip of ancient papyrus shows a richly detailed funerary scene and columns of hieroglyphic text. Overall, the piece conveys the ceremonial language and iconography of ancient Egyptian burial literature and ritual practice.
